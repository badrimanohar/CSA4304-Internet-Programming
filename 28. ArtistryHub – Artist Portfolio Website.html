About the Artist

Discover the story behind Emily Harris's creativity.

Emily Harris

Emily Harris is a contemporary artist known for her expressive paintings, abstract compositions and experimental approach to color and texture.

Her work explores human emotions, nature, memories and the relationship between people and their surroundings.

Emily has participated in numerous exhibitions and art festivals and her work has been displayed in galleries across different cities.

Born: 1988
Profession: Contemporary Artist
Specialization: Painting & Mixed Media
Experience: 15+ Years
