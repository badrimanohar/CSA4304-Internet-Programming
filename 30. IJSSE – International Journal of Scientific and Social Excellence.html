About the Journal

Promoting quality research in scientific and social disciplines.

Our Mission

The International Journal of Scientific and Social Excellence aims to provide a professional platform for researchers to publish high-quality research and exchange innovative ideas.


The journal supports transparent academic publishing, peer review, research integrity and global knowledge sharing.

Journal Scope

IJSSE welcomes original research papers, review articles, case studies and innovative academic work in the areas of:


Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Engineering, Social Science, Management, Education, Technology and Interdisciplinary Research.

Publication Ethics

Maintaining integrity, transparency and ethical research practices.

1. Originality

Submitted manuscripts must contain original research work and should not be published elsewhere.

2. Plagiarism Policy

All submitted articles are expected to follow academic integrity standards and avoid plagiarism.

3. Confidentiality

Editors and reviewers must maintain the confidentiality of manuscripts during the review process.

4. Fair Peer Review

Articles are evaluated based on research quality, originality and relevance without unfair discrimination.

5. Conflict of Interest

Authors, reviewers and editors should disclose conflicts that may influence the publication process.
