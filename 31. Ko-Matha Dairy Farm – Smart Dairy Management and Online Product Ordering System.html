About Ko-Matha Dairy Farm

Traditional dairy values supported by modern technology.

Healthy Cows. Pure Milk. Happy Families.

Ko-Matha Dairy Farm is committed to providing high-quality dairy products sourced from healthy Indian A1 cow holders.

Our farm uses modern technology for quality monitoring, stock management, cow maintenance and efficient customer delivery.

From fresh milk to curd, butter, cova sweet and ghee, every product is handled with care and delivered with freshness.
