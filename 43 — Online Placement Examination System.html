Online Placement Examination

A secure online examination platform for conducting placement examinations, evaluating candidates automatically and publishing examination results.


System Features

📝 Online Examination

Candidates can attend scheduled placement examinations through an online examination dashboard.

⏰ Exam Scheduling

Admin can schedule examinations and provide examination access to candidates.

⚡ Automatic Evaluation

Answers are evaluated automatically and the candidate's score is generated immediately.

🏆 Ranking

Candidate scores can be compared to generate examination rankings.

📊 Result Management

Candidates can view their scores, pass/fail status and performance details.

🔄 Re-evaluation

Candidates can submit a re-evaluation request if they have concerns about their result.
